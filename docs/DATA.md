## Updating safely

1. Check the owning source and date, including original labels, units and definitions.
2. Do not infer minimum investment from share price or valuation.
3. Preserve platform metrics; do not normalise incompatible returns into a single ranking.
4. Keep country and offering restrictions separate. An account country dropdown is not legal approval.
5. Confirm market availability; a listed token is not guaranteed liquidity.
6. Record verified changes in reviewable commits. Update capture dates only for records actually checked.
7. Keep missing facts explicit. Never silently fill unknown fees with zero.
8. Never add capital custody or investment execution to this frontend.

## Rights

Listing/photo display is based on the owner's reported platform approval, subject to preserving core metrics and handling no capital on-site. No transferable licence is granted to repository visitors. Check the platform or rights holder before downstream reuse.
